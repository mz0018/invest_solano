PROCESS OF AVAILING INCENTIVES

Source: 7.png

PROCESS OF AVAILING INCENTIVES

Filing of Application
The application for incentives should be filed within thirty (30) days from the issuance of the Mayor’s permit or within the period stated in the ordinance. Forms and documentary requirements must be submitted to the Municipal Investment Office.

Assessment of Documents
The municipal office shall assess the completeness of the documentary requirements and supporting documents.

Site Inspection / Assessment
The municipal office or authorized officers shall conduct a site inspection for compliance.

Evaluation and Approval
The Investment Promotion Board (or appropriate LGU body) will evaluate the project and make a recommendation based on compliance with local ordinances and development goals.

Order of Payment / Issuance of Incentives
Upon approval, an order of payment and certificate of registration will be issued. Incentives shall be accorded as prescribed in the local ordinance.

Monitoring and Compliance
Approved enterprises may be subject to monitoring to ensure continued compliance with the terms of incentives. Wrongful claim or misuse can lead to revocation.
